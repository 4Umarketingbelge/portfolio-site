Questions

Who holds my funds?

Only you. NovaLedger is non-custodial: keys are generated and stored on your device.

What if I lose my device?

Restore your wallet anywhere with your recovery phrase. Keep it offline and never share it with anyone.

Are there fees?

Network fees go to the network. Any service fee is displayed before you confirm a swap.
