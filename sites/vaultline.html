Questions
Can Vaultline move my funds?

No. It reads public blockchain data only and has no permission to send or sign anything.

Which assets are supported?

Major coins, stablecoins and the most common tokens on the supported networks.

Is this a real product?

No. Vaultline is a fictional concept made as a design sample, and every figure on this page is example data.

Design concept for portfolio use. Not financial advice.
